I'm a software engineer who likes building things that are fast, precise, and a little bit weird. I spend most of my time thinking about <span class="hover-text" data-image="/images/malloc.jpeg">systems</span>: how they break, how they scale, and how to make them simpler without losing the interesting parts.

Outside of writing code, I'm usually reading about things I don't fully understand yet, listening to <span class="hover-text" data-image="/images/listening.gif">music</span> that doesn't quite fit one genre, or watching <span class="hover-text" data-image="/images/anniyan-ambi-remo.jpg">films</span> that take their time. I believe the best ideas come from crossing disciplines that have no obvious business being near each other.

This site is where I put thoughts that don't fit anywhere else: rough notes, longer essays, and the occasional <span class="hover-text" data-image="/images/sidequest.jpeg">rabbit hole</span> I fell into and couldn't climb back out of. Nothing here is final.
